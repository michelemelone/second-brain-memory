### MIC-535 Final Report: Responsive Layout, Touch & Pointer Column Resizing, Resizable AI Drawer, and Adaptive Header

#### 1. What was done
- **Pointer and touch events for left sidebar** (`web/components/app-shell.tsx`):
  - Replaced `onMouseDown` with `onPointerDown` utilizing `setPointerCapture` on the resize handle.
  - Added `touch-none` and `select-none` to guarantee reliable touch drag behavior on mobile and foldable screens (e.g. Galaxy Z Fold 7 folded ~380px and unfolded ~880px) and mouse dragging on desktop.
- **Resizable right AI drawer** (`web/components/ai-drawer.tsx`):
  - Added a draggable divider on the left edge of the desktop/docked drawer with pointer and touch events (`setPointerCapture`).
  - Implemented dynamic drawer resizing within constraints (`min 260px`, `max 500px`, default `340px`) and persisted the width in `localStorage` under `finance:ai-drawer-width`.
  - Removed transition lag during active dragging (`isDragging || isAiDragging`), allowing `--ai-w` and container padding to smoothly adapt with zero layout lag. Added `pointer-events-none` on the embedded iframe while dragging to prevent event swallowing.
- **Responsive and overflow-proof header** (`web/components/app-shell.tsx`):
  - Grouped right-side controls (`Columns3`, `AIDrawerToggle`, `Sign out`, `Theme`) with `shrink-0` to ensure they never wrap, overflow off-screen, or get obscured behind the docked AI drawer.
  - Made the search bar flexible (`max-w-[180px]`) and collapsible on small viewports with an expandable search icon button toggle, ensuring seamless usability from 360px up to 1400px+.
- **Adaptive auto-fit and viewport budget** (`web/components/app-shell.tsx`):
  - Enhanced the `Columns3` button logic to evaluate window viewport width (`window.innerWidth`).
  - On viewport widths below 1000px with the AI drawer open, it ensures the central dashboard maintains at least 450px breathing room by collapsing the left sidebar to icon mode (64px) when necessary and sizing the AI drawer to its compact width (260px).
- **Double-click reset on dividers**:
  - Double-clicking the left sidebar divider resets its width to 190px and expands if collapsed.
  - Double-clicking the AI drawer divider resets its width to 340px.
- **Build and deployment**:
  - Ran `npm run build` in `personal-finance/web`, compiling cleanly with code 0 without TypeScript or lint errors.
  - Queued service restart for `Finance app` using `bigmike-restart`.
