ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
1. Gantry's frontend holds its own picker code: the picker implementation and its styles live inside `gantry/programs/servicenow_ai/gantry/frontend/src/components/askAi/` (and `index.css` for the theme), and no file of Gantry's frontend imports anything from bigMike's checkout. 2. Gantry builds from a clone that has no bigMike checkout next to it: `npm ci && npm run build` succeeds in a temporary clone of the Gantry repo placed outside `/home/beedme/data/workspace` (or with the `personal-assistant` path made unreachable), proving the build no longer depends on bigMike's working tree. Verified by quoting the command and its output in the final report.
3. A change in bigMike's picker cannot reach Gantry by itself: after editing a visible string in `personal-assistant/programs/web/src/components/ModelPicker.tsx` (reverted immediately afterwards) and rebuilding only bigMike, Gantry's built app still shows its own text. Verified by the two builds and the screenshots.
4. Gantry keeps every picker function it has today, on its own code: engine, model and effort choice; default agent; the ordered fallback chain (fallback 1..6) saved through Gantry's own endpoints `/api/ask-ai/engine-choices*`; usage; Compact and Clear; the icon variant used in the backlog table; the composer and pill variants. Verified by Gantry's test suite green (tests quoted by name) and by screenshots of the open picker in each variant.
5. Gantry's look does not change in this task: the picker keeps `.bmp-root[data-bm-theme="gantry"]` and its `--bmp-*` mapping, identical to what is live today. Verified by before/after screenshots in light and dark.
6. Gantry's settings are its own: the default model and the ordered fallback chain are read from and written to Gantry's server only, and no screen reads bigMike's settings at runtime. Verified by the network log of the settings screens (no request to a bigMike host) and by a test that saves a chain and reads it back.
7. The copy starts from the picker as restored by MIC-540 and laid out by MIC-582 (per-row star and menu, search, favourites, Compact/Clear in the top row, fallback positions on the row), so Gantry is not left on the broken layout; from then on the two may diverge only when the owner asks. Verified by the diff against bigMike's restored component and by screenshots.
8. English only; the contract `personal-assistant/programs/docs/model-picker.md` and the ENGINE PICKER rule in `COMMON_RULES` name Gantry as the one application with its own copy, so no later task tries to re-share it. Verified by the doc and rule diff.
9. Gantry's process is followed: build, tests, technical lessons recorded in `build_agent/skills/servicenow-development/lessons.md`, never production or UAT, restart queued with `bigmike-restart Gantry` (never a direct kill); the final report names the commits. Verified by the report.
10. Every criterion has its evidence in the final report, with a screenshot of the picker open on desktop, in light and dark, and at 390 px.
