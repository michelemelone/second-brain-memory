## Last report of the agent

The "Describe what you want" side panel and its header toggle are built, but I haven't looked at them in a browser, so the layout and contrast are unchecked. The commit is `8c3300a`, pushed to `main`.

**What it does**
- **Toggle:** a button at the right of the header, with the panel icon and a "+" speech bubble as in the Lab, opens the panel from any page. It shows a pressed state while the panel is open, and the panel closes with the X.
- **Panel:** a 360px panel on the right of the page, titled "Describe what you want", using Gantry's existing message box (including dictation).
- **Sending:** your text becomes a new requirement in the Backlog.
  - The title is the first line, cut at about 90 characters.
  - The full text is the description.
  - The requirement goes to the Global application, with no application chosen.
  - A link to the Backlog appears after it is created.
- **Draft:** the draft and the open or closed state are kept in the browser (localStorage). They survive page changes and reloads, and the draft is cleared only after a successful send. If creating fails, an error shows and the draft stays.
- **Separate copy:** nothing is imported from bigMike. All text is in English.

**What I did not do**
- I did not copy the Lab's multi-task planning run, its questions step or its attachments. Gantry has no equivalent endpoint, so this is a plain "wish to requirement".
- I did not restart Gantry. The frontend build is done, but I assume it is served from `dist` and the service has not been restarted, so I haven't confirmed the new panel is live.
