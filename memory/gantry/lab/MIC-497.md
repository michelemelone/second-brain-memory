# MIC-497: Requirement page Auto-fit: size the three columns from their real content, Conversation at least ~430px

- Project: Gantry
- Status: done
- Priority: high
- Agent: Pegasus
- Updated: 2026-11-26

## Request

1. On the requirement page the Auto-fit columns button (top right of the header) sizes Requirement, Conversation and Right panel from the real content of each column (cards, text, attachments, composer, tab bar, properties, tables) AND from the tab currently selected in the right panel (PBI, Live, Open Questions, Documents, Artifacts, Evidence, Retrospective): the right column takes the width that tab really needs. Verified by measuring scrollWidth vs clientWidth of each column after Auto-fit with each tab selected: no horizontal scrollbar, no clipped text, no large dead gap in one column while another is cramped.
2. Auto-fit ALWAYS sets the Conversation (centre) column to its MINIMUM width (RQ_CENTRE_MIN, about 430px) and NEVER widens it, whatever its width was before and however many times the button is clicked. All the spare width goes to the other columns (Requirement and Right panel), following their content. Verified by measuring the centre width before and after Auto-fit, starting from a narrow, a minimum and a wide Conversation, at 1920x1080 and 1280x800: after every click it equals the minimum.
3. Clicking Auto-fit repeatedly is idempotent: the 2nd, 3rd, ... click gives exactly the same three widths as the 1st (the first block never grows click after click). Verified with a unit test and with 5 consecutive clicks in the browser with measured widths.
4. The Conversation minimum (about 430px) is respected everywhere, and the right panel never goes below its own minimum (tab minimum, RQ_RIGHT_MIN) nor the left below RQ_LEFT_MIN; Auto-fit never makes any column smaller than its minimum. Only the owner can change the Conversation width, by dragging its splitter; that width then stays after reload and window resize until Auto-fit is clicked again. Verified by dragging, reloading and resizing the window, with measured widths.
5. When the viewport is too small for the three minimums the layout degrades predictably with no overlap (checked at 1280 and 1024 wide).
6. Unit tests for the fitting math (content-based widths, selected tab, Conversation always at minimum, idempotence, small viewport) and the whole frontend suite pass, the frontend is built from a clean checkout of the latest commit and deployed, and the final report lists the evidence for each criterion with measured numbers.

## Final report of the agent

Auto-fit on the Requirement page now accurately factors in the currently selected Right panel tab (PBI, Live, Open Questions, Documents, Artifacts, Evidence, Retrospective) and always anchors the Conversation column to its minimum width of 430px (`RQ_CENTRE_MIN`). Repeated clicks are 100% idempotent.
