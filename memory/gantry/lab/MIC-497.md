## Request

1. On the requirement page the Auto-fit columns button (top right of the header) sizes Requirement, Conversation and Right panel from the real content of each column (cards, text, attachments, composer, tab bar, properties, tables in the Artifacts / Documents / Evidence tabs), not only from the three main containers. Verified by measuring scrollWidth vs clientWidth of each column after Auto-fit on the PBI, Live, Open Questions, Documents, Artifacts, Evidence and Retrospective tabs: no horizontal scrollbar and no clipped text caused by the distribution, and no large dead gap in a column while another is cramped.
2. After Auto-fit the Conversation column is never narrower than about 430px (the reference width in the owner's screenshot: messages, 'Validate again' header, answer box and its buttons all fit on one row, no word-by-word wrapping). Verified with a screenshot at 1920x1080 and 1280x800 and a measured width. The old 190px lower bound and the 25% ceiling no longer apply when they make it narrower than this; the Conversation may take up to what the other columns can spare.
3. The same minimum holds when dragging the splitters and on page reload and window resize (stored layout is clamped to it). Verified by dragging each splitter to its limit and reloading.
4. When the viewport is too small for all three minimums, the layout degrades predictably (right panel shrinks last-resort first to its tab minimum, then horizontal scroll or the existing under-1024px mobile layout) with no overlap. Verified at 1280x800 and 1024 wide.
5. Unit tests for the fitting math (content-based widths, Conversation minimum, small viewport) pass, the whole frontend suite passes, the frontend is built and deployed, and the final report lists the evidence for each criterion with measured numbers.

Context: owner screenshots on PBI #206 (Paws & Co Animal): after Auto-fit the Conversation is about 130px wide, text wraps almost per word, while the right panel is wide and half empty. Related done tasks: MIC-375 (columns fill the width, chat capped at 25%, fitRequirementColumns / leftDragRange / rightDragMax in frontend/src/lib/panelFit.ts and components/requirement/RequirementWorkspace.tsx), MIC-387, MIC-460.
