## Last report of the agent

The two Gantry composers now share one rectangular box, but I couldn't view the screenshots, so I haven't checked it by eye. It's committed as `5ac16d6` and pushed to `main`.

**What changed**
- Both composers already used `ChatInput.tsx`, so I redesigned that one Gantry-local component. It has no bigMike imports.
- The box is full-width and rectangular. The text area sits on top and a button bar sits inside the bottom. In the bar, the model picker is on the left. On the right are the Enter-to-send switch, the mic, Stop (while the assistant is answering) and Send.
- I added a `variant` prop. `narrow` has a taller text area and is used by the Describe panel (`DescribePanel.tsx`). `wide` is the default and is used by the requirement chat ("Validate with AI").
- Voice, send, the draft being kept and the "Changes are only applied when you click Apply" note are unchanged.
- I added a transparent native `placeholder` on the text area. The existing `RequirementFormModal` tests look for that attribute, and the visible placeholder is still drawn separately.
