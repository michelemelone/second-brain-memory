# Protein intake versus cost

Question: how does protein intake relate to what it costs?

## Short answer
With today's summaries only a rough monthly comparison is possible, not a real cost per gram of protein. Two months overlap: 2026-10 and 2026-11 (partial month).

## Where to read each side
- Protein: average g/day in the `## Nutrition` table of [2026-10](../memory/health/2026-10.md) and [2026-11](../memory/health/2026-11.md). Days logged is stated there; 2026-11 covers only part of the month.
- Cost: `Food` rows under `## Spending by category` in [finance 2026-10](../memory/finance/2026-10.md) and [finance 2026-11](../memory/finance/2026-11.md), plus the supermarket rows in the top merchants table.

## Rough method (do the arithmetic from the linked files, do not store it here)
1. Protein per month = average protein g/day x days logged (Health summary).
2. Food spending per month = Food category per currency (Finance summary).
3. Rough cost per 100 g of protein = Food spending / protein grams x 100, per currency, never mixing CHF and EUR.

## Why the result is only indicative
- Food mixes protein with everything else (bread, pasta, wine, restaurants such as pizzerias and trattorias); no per-product cost exists.
- Amounts are in CHF and EUR and are not converted; a Swiss and an Italian/German shop cannot be added without a rate.
- Cash and unlogged purchases are missing; meals logged are estimates by weight.
- Nutrition logging starts on 2026-10-05, so there is no earlier baseline.
- Eating out is in the Food total but its protein is estimated in the log, so both sides drift.
