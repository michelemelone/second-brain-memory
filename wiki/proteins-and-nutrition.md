# Proteins and Nutrition

Hub page: where protein and nutrition data live, and how they connect to food spending. Not medical advice; nobody here is a licensed professional.

## Where the numbers live (never copied here)
| What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Intake per day (kcal, protein, carbs, fat) | Monthly `## Nutrition` table in [2026-10](../memory/health/2026-10.md) and [2026-11](../memory/health/2026-11.md) | Monthly averages only. Earlier months, including 2026-09, have no meals logged. Raw rows stay in the Health app. |
| Weight and jab (tirzepatide) | Same Health summaries, `## Weight` and `## Jab` | Context for low appetite and protein targets. |
| What he actually eats, targets, daily logs | [[meal-logging]] in the bigMike wiki: `../memory/bigmike/wiki/topics/meal-logging.md` | Protein target stated there: 120-150 g/day. Logging started 2026-10-05. |
| Weight-loss context | `../memory/bigmike/wiki/topics/mounjaro-weight-loss.md` | |
| Food spending | `## Spending by category` (Food) and `## Top merchants` in the monthly files listed in [finance index](../memory/finance/index.md) | Amounts per currency (CHF and EUR), not converted. Raw transactions stay in the Finance app. |

## How the two sides connect
- Health knows grams of protein per day, but not what it cost.
- Finance knows what was spent on food (category Food, supermarkets such as Aldi, Lidl, EDEKA), but not what was bought.
- The link is the month: one protein average per month against one Food total per month. See [[protein-intake-vs-cost]].

## Recurring protein sources (from the meal log)
Whey shake, high-protein cottage cheese and yogurt cake (Aldi), chicken, cod, lean beef, lentils. Source: [[meal-logging]].
